About Luxe Men Salon

A Sanctuary for the
Distinguished Man

Started in October 2022, Luxe Men Salon is positioned as more than a barbershop — a sanctuary for the distinguished man. A place where ritual replaces routine: precision cuts, hot towel shaves, and sophisticated, masculine environments crafted around one standard — excellence.

Behind the artistry sits a discipline most salons never master: strong unit economics. Every square foot is designed to work, every model engineered to profit. That is why since our start in October 2022, we are expanding rapidly with partners who share our obsession.
